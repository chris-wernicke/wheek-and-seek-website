5. Anonymous play-flow counter

So that we can tell whether the game loads well and where players stop, the game reports to our server (wheek-mp.denkgold.de) which stages were reached during a visit (e.g. “game loaded”, “round started”, “round cleared”, “played for 3 minutes”, “left in the menu”, time of day in 3-hour blocks), together with the game version, the game portal and whether it runs on a touch device. Only totals per day, game version, portal and device type are stored. There is no identifier, no cookie and nothing is stored on your device; individual players or devices cannot be told apart. Your IP address is transmitted for technical reasons, counted only briefly in memory to prevent abuse (requests per minute) and neither stored nor logged. The totals are deleted after 30 days.

If “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” is switched on in your browser, the game sends nothing.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the game and detecting loading problems). You can object at any time (Art. 21 GDPR), e.g. via the browser settings mentioned.
